About Marvel Rivals

Assemble a squad from across the Marvel universe and enter fast-moving team battles where positioning, hero combinations and environmental destruction can change the match in seconds.

  • Choose from an all-star roster of Marvel heroes and villains.
  • Combine character powers to activate distinctive Team-Up abilities.
  • Fight in team-based online PVP with cooperative squad play.
  • Use destructible environments to open routes and reshape the battlefield.
  • Return to an evolving Marvel universe with new content and strategies.
